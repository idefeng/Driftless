## 中文商店信息

应用名称（≤30）：Driftless 跑步步频节拍器

副标题：一拍一步，跑出稳定步频

简短说明（≤80）：
跑步步频节拍器：一拍一步，零漂移音频时钟，支持间歇计划、渐进变速、边听音乐边跑。

详细介绍：
Driftless 是一款为跑者打造的步频节拍器——一拍一步，帮你把步频稳稳钉住。

节拍直接由硬件音频时钟按采样精度排布，连续跑上几个小时也不会越跑越快或越跑越慢。打开即用，无需账号，不联网，所有数据只保存在你的手机上。

【一拍一步】
首页的左右脚印跟着节拍交替落地，跑步时扫一眼就能对上节奏。

【间歇训练，自动换段】
自由组合热身、巡航、冲刺等阶段，每段独立设置时长和步频。
· 渐进过渡：换段前 10 / 20 / 30 秒平滑加减速到下一段步频，不再突然变奏。
· 换段提示音：每段结束前 3 秒倒数，提示音对齐拍点，不打乱节奏。
· 计划可通过二维码分享给跑友。

【边听音乐边跑】
节拍叠加在你正在听的音乐、播客之上，绝不打断、绝不暂停其他 App；需要专注时也可切换为独占模式。节拍音量独立于媒体音量调节。

【5 种音色 + 重音拍】
电子嘀声、木鱼、标准节拍器、气泡、水滴，嘈杂路边也听得清。重音拍可每 2 / 4 拍加一个强拍，帮你感知左右脚节奏。

【为跑步中的盲操设计】
占满屏幕下方的超大 ±1 按钮，长按连续调节；播放时在步频区域上下滑动也能微调。锁屏通知中同样可以 ±1、跳过阶段。

【夜跑与强光都清晰】
黑底荧光绿高对比界面；户外强光下可切换「日光强光」白底模式。支持屏幕常亮。

适合：想提高或稳定步频的跑者、节奏跑 / 间歇跑 / 马拉松备赛人群，以及希望用声音代替频繁看表的人。

## Google Play English Listing

Title (≤30):
Driftless: Running Cadence

Short description (≤80):
One beat, one step. Zero-drift running cadence metronome with interval plans.

Full description:
Driftless is a cadence metronome built for runners — one beat, one step, locked in.

Every beat is placed by the hardware audio clock with sample accuracy, so your rhythm never creeps faster or slower, even over hours. No account, no network, and everything stays on your phone.

ONE BEAT, ONE STEP
Footprints on the home screen land left and right in time with the beat — one glance and you're back in rhythm.

INTERVALS THAT SHIFT FOR YOU
Build workouts from warm-up, cruise and sprint phases, each with its own duration and cadence.
• Gradual ramps: glide smoothly to the next phase's cadence over 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
• Phase-change cue: a 3-second countdown before each phase ends, landing right on the beat.
• Share plans with friends via QR code.

KEEP YOUR MUSIC PLAYING
The beat layers on top of your music or podcast and never pauses other apps. Switch to exclusive mode when you want to focus. Beat volume is independent of media volume.

5 SOUNDS + ACCENT BEATS
Electronic beep, wood block, classic click, bubble and droplet — clear even next to traffic. Add an accent every 2 or 4 beats to feel your left and right foot.

MADE FOR BLIND CONTROL
Huge ±1 buttons fill the bottom of the screen; hold to repeat, or swipe on the cadence while playing. The lock-screen notification has ±1 and skip-phase controls too.

CLEAR AT NIGHT AND IN THE SUN
High-contrast black and volt-green design, plus a white "Solar" mode for bright outdoor light. Keep-screen-awake included.

Great for runners raising or steadying their cadence, tempo and interval sessions, marathon training, and anyone who'd rather listen than keep checking their watch.
